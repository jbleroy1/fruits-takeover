SONIC 4SHATTERED HORIZON

Dr. Eggman shattered the sky into 17 zones. Pick your hero and run, fly, glide or hammer your way through 102 acts and 50 branch levels. Collect rings, smash badniks, free the animals and find the 7 Chaos Emeralds — then go Super for the two Doomsday levels and tear Eggman's giant robot apart.

HOW TO PLAY

MoveA / D or ← / → JumpSpace, W, ↑, Z or K Roll / crouchS or ↓ (roll while running) AttackX, J or Shift Second abilityJump again in mid-air Spin dashhold ↓ + tap Jump, then release ↓ (Sonic) PauseP or Esc Restart levelR MuteM Tablet / phoneon-screen buttons, | | pauses, [ ] is fullscreen

Rings are your health: get hit with rings and you drop them, get hit with none and you lose a life. Break the item monitors for shields, speed shoes, invincibility and extra lives. Hit a checkpoint and you restart from there. With all 7 Chaos Emeralds and 50 rings, press Attack in mid-air to go Super.

All four are playable in every one of the 152 levels. Switch any time from the zone map.
