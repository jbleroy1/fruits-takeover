THE ORCHARD TOOK THE WORLD

It began with one apple that would not be picked.

Armies went into the groves with flame and steel. They fell. Not quickly — that is the worst of it. Fruit does not bite and finish you. It touches you, and leaves rot behind, and waits while the rot does the work. Whole battalions walked out of the trees three days later, soft and sweet and no longer ours.

You are the last Juicer. You have a pressure gun, a dash, and a very long afternoon.
Keep moving. Shake the rot off. Make juice.

HOW TO PLAY

WASD or arrows — move
Mouse — aim, hold click — fire
Space — fire where you face
Shift — dash: brief invincibility, shakes off rot
P / Esc — pause
M — mute
Phone / tablet: the D-pad, FIRE and DASH buttons appear on screen, with | | to pause and [ ] for fullscreen
Touch, the free way: drag the left half to move, hold the right half to aim and fire
Rot builds on contact and eats your life until you dash it off

Fruit rarely kills you outright. It lands rot on you and walks away. Salt packets clear rot, hearts heal, golden crates upgrade the gun mid-level.

  Keyboard + mouse, or touch: on a phone or tablet the D-pad, FIRE and DASH buttons appear by themselves. Sound starts after your first tap.
